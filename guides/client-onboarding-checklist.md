# Client Onboarding Checklist for Freelancers (10 Steps)

*The first week after a client says "yes" determines how the whole project goes. Run this checklist every time — never rely on memory.*

## The 10 steps

1. **Signed agreement received and filed.** No signature, no work. No exceptions — the exceptions are where the horror stories come from.
2. **Deposit invoice sent → paid.** Work starts *after* the deposit clears, not before. 50% is standard for fixed-price projects.
3. **Scope of Work confirmed in writing.** Deliverables, revision rounds, and — just as important — what's explicitly *not* included.
4. **Client questionnaire sent → answers received.** Goals, audience, examples they like, what they *don't* want, and who makes final decisions.
5. **Kickoff call held.** 30 minutes: roles, the goal in one sentence, milestones, communication rhythm.
6. **Recap email sent same day.** Every decision, every open item with an owner and a date. Verbal agreements are where disputes are born.
7. **Materials and logins collected.** Brand assets, copy, photos, access — listed in the recap so nothing is "assumed."
8. **Project folder + milestones on your calendar.** Including THEIR feedback deadlines, not just yours.
9. **First status update scheduled.** Same day every week, even when there's little to report. Rhythm makes clients relax; silence makes them anxious.
10. **Final invoice drafted and dated.** Set it up on day one so nothing is forgotten at the end.

## The welcome email that ties it together

```
Subject: Welcome aboard — here's how we'll start

Hi [First name],

Thrilled to be working with you on [project]. Here's everything
you need to know for a smooth start:

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
1. Attached: the agreement and the invoice for the deposit.
2. Once those are in, I'll send the kickoff questionnaire
   (takes about 15 minutes).
3. Then we'll schedule a [30]-minute kickoff call to align on
   goals before I start.

HOW WE'LL COMMUNICATE
• Email for anything that needs a record ([my email])
• [Weekly status update every Friday]
• I reply within [one business day]

TIMELINE
We're on track for [final delivery date]. Full milestone list
is in the attached Scope of Work.

Any questions, just reply here. Let's build something great.

[Your name]
```

## Why this matters more than talent

A structured onboarding makes a $500 freelancer look like a $5,000 agency — and it prevents the three most common project killers: unclear goals, missing materials, and "I thought you knew."
