# Late Payment Reminder Emails: The 3-Strike Sequence for Freelancers

*Chasing invoices is the worst part of freelancing. This 3-email sequence collects most late payments without damaging the relationship.*

## The principle

Escalate in **firmness**, never in **rudeness**. Most late invoices are admin failures, not malice — until proven otherwise. Each email assumes slightly less innocence than the last.

## Reminder 1 — 1 day overdue: the friendly flag

```
Hi [Name],

Invoice #[number] for [$X] was due yesterday — just flagging
in case it got lost in the shuffle. Payment link is here for
convenience: [link].

Thanks!
```

90% of the time, it slipped. Short, zero accusation, easy to act on.

## Reminder 2 — 7 days overdue: direct, still warm

```
Hi [Name],

Following up on invoice #[number] ([$X]), now a week past due.
Is there anything blocking payment on your end? If there's an
issue I should know about, tell me and we'll sort it out.
Otherwise I'd appreciate payment by [date, 3 days out].

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

Opens a door for a genuine problem (lost invoice, approver on leave) while naming a deadline.

## Reminder 3 — 14 days overdue: invoke the agreement

```
Hi [Name],

Invoice #[number] ([$X]) is now 14 days overdue. Per our
agreement dated [date], [late fees now apply / I'll need to
pause all work] as of [date].

I'd much rather resolve this between us — please let me know
today when payment will be made, or contact me to work out
a plan.

[Your name]
```

Formal, factual, references the contract. No anger — the document does the talking.

## If it goes past 30 days

A final written notice stating you'll pursue formal collection (small claims court or a collections service) resolves most remaining cases. But the real defense is upstream: **deposits before work starts** mean you're rarely owed more than half the project value.

## Prevention (worth more than any reminder)

- 50% deposit before work begins, non-refundable
- Late-fee clause in every agreement (e.g., 1.5%/month)
- Work-pause clause for invoices 14+ days late
- Ownership of deliverables transfers only on full payment
