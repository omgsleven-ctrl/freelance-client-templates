# The Follow-Up (When a Prospect Goes Quiet)

**When to use:** you sent a proposal or quote and heard nothing. Send 4–5 business days after your last message. Once more a week later. Then stop.

## The script

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [First name],

Just floating this back up in case it got buried — I know how
inboxes get.

If the timing's off, no problem at all. And if priorities have
shifted, I'm happy to point you toward [a resource / someone
else] instead.

Either way, wishing you luck with [their goal].

[Your name]
```

## Why it works

Two follow-ups maximum, always friendly, never guilt-tripping. The "no problem at all" line removes the social debt of not answering — and paradoxically gets more replies than pressure ever does.
