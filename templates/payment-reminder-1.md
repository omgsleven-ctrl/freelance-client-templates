# Payment Reminder 1 (The Friendly Flag)

**When to use:** the invoice is 1 day overdue. Assume it slipped — 90% of the time it did.

## The script

```
Hi [Name],

Invoice #[number] for [$X] was due yesterday — just flagging
in case it got lost in the shuffle. Payment link is here for
convenience: [link].

Thanks!
```

## Escalation

- **Day 7 overdue:** direct but warm — ask if anything is blocking payment; request payment by a specific date 3 days out.
- **Day 14 overdue:** invoke the agreement — reference the contract date, late terms, and that work pauses until the account is current. Firm, never rude.

Escalate in firmness, never in rudeness. (The full 3-strike sequence with all three scripts is in The Freelance Client Kit.)
